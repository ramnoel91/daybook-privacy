Daybook Calendar Privacy Policy

Last updated October 29, 2026

Daybook Calendar does not collect, share or sell any personal data. Everything you enter stays on your phone. The app has no account, no ads, no analytics and no internet access.

What the app stores

Daybook saves your events, birthdays, to-dos, habits, bills, day notes, checklists, settings and any wallpaper pictures you add. This information is stored only in the app's private storage on your device. The developer cannot see it, and it is never sent to any server.

Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in Android settings, permanently deletes this information.

Backups and sharing

You can save a backup file or share an event as text. Daybook does this only when you tap the button, and the file or text goes only where you choose to send it.

Permissions

PermissionWhy Daybook uses it
NotificationsShows your event reminders and morning summary
Alarms and remindersDelivers reminders at the exact time you set
Run at startupSets your reminders again after the phone restarts
VibrationVibrates when a reminder arrives

When you add a wallpaper or restore a backup, Android's file picker gives Daybook access only to the files you pick.

Links to other services

The Add to Google Calendar button opens Google Calendar in your browser with the event's details filled in. What happens there is covered by Google's privacy policy.

Children

Daybook is not directed at children under 13 and collects no information from anyone.

Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
